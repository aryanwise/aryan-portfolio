Engineered a competitive AI game agent capable of playing Gomoku (Five-in-a-Row) against both human players and opponent algorithms within a strict 10-second turn limit:

  • Search efficiently. Use depth-variable minimax with alpha-beta pruning and iterative deepening, respecting a 10-second cutoff per turn.
  • Limit the branching. Consider moves near existing stones instead of evaluating every empty space on the board.
  • Score the threats. Apply positional heuristics to five-in-a-row, open and blocked fours, and other sequences across horizontal, vertical and diagonal lines.
